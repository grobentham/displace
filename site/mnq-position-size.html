MNQ position sizing

An MNQ contract is worth US$2 per point. Multiply your stop distance by $2 and add round-trip fees and estimated slippage per contract.

Example

A 20-point stop and $3 in estimated costs comes to $43 risk per contract. A $100 trade budget and $150 remaining allowance cover two whole contracts at that estimate.
