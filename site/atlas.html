About the database

Annual observations from World Bank World Development Indicators, source 2. This is not a real-time economic monitor or a forecast. Profiles show the latest available observation with its year; comparisons use the same selected year for every economy. Missing values are never estimated or forward-filled.

Source health

Retrieval time does not establish observation release time. A recent download may contain older annual data. Data may be revised by the provider.

Methodological limitations

GDP in current dollars depends on exchange rates. Central government debt excludes other public-sector debt and has uneven coverage. Unemployment is a modeled ILO estimate, not necessarily a national headline rate. Annual indicators do not measure current policy rates or short-term financial risk. No composite country score or investment recommendation is implied. Review source-specific data usage rights before commercial redistribution.
