Editorial policy

Every edition is generated from the existing quantitative engines using fixed templates, not generative AI. The first daily candidate after noon UTC is archived. Signal reports correspond to recorded transitions; research reports correspond to completed registered batches. Weekly and monthly reviews are frozen as-of snapshots, not final settlement claims. An unsuccessful or missed run does not fabricate an edition. Source links may show later data; source hashes record the generation-time inputs.
