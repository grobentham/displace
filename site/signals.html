Monitoring methodology

Alerts describe current conditions, not future market direction. A condition activates at its entry threshold and clears only after reaching its separate exit threshold. A first detection is not necessarily a new crossing. Missing or stale inputs suspend evaluation without declaring the condition resolved.

Limitations

These thresholds are fixed monitoring policy choices, not independently validated predictive rules. Components and indices overlap, so alerts are not independent. Current-vintage data can be revised. The system does not infer causes, recommend trades or claim predictive accuracy.
