How to read the scores

Each component is ranked against its own prior observations. The index is an equally weighted average of those component percentiles. Higher values indicate more of the named condition. These are descriptive scores, not event probabilities or trading recommendations.

Limitations

Current-vintage data may be revised. Scores are unavailable when any required component is missing, stale or lacks enough historical observations. Components overlap across indices, so the six scores are not independent. The monetary conditions score is a proxy, not a direct measure of funding liquidity. No historical predictive validation is implied.
