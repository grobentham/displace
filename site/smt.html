How it is measured

Swing divergence. A swing high is a bar whose high is the highest of 3 bars either side (2 on hourly bars). At each swing high, Displace compares each market's high around it with its high around the previous swing high, up to 4 hours earlier (24 hours hourly). If one made a higher high and the other did not, that is a bearish divergence. Swing lows give bullish divergences. A divergence is only known once the swing is confirmed, 3 bars later, and outcomes are measured from that bar.

Level divergence. When one market trades through its previous-day, Asia or London high or low and the other still has not 3 bars later.

Limits. Bars are delayed Yahoo Finance futures data, updated when the Displace engine runs (not tick by tick). Continuous contracts jump at rolls. 5-minute history starts in September 2026 and grows every day, so most 5-minute groups have too few cases to judge yet. This is a measurement of the past, not a trade signal or advice.
