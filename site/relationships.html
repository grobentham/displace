Cross-market monitoring · Matched observation intervals · Descriptive statistical analysis

Methodology

Returns and changes are aligned by both their starting and ending observation dates. Rolling correlations use the latest 20, 60 or 252 matched observations, not calendar days. A correlation shift compares two disjoint 60-observation windows. The residual diagnostic regresses the second series on the first using only the preceding 60 matched observations. It measures how unusual the latest relationship is, not a future price prediction.

Limitations

Correlation does not imply causation. These are current-vintage descriptive relationships, affected by different market closing times, data publication lags, revisions, missing observations and futures rolls. Threshold flags are monitoring choices, not significance tests or validated trading opportunities. The fitted residual is contemporaneous and is not a forecast. Stale pairs have no current metrics.
