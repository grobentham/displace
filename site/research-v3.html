Research discovery

Version 3 · 128 registered tests · corrected method · every result kept

What changed from versions 1 and 2

Versions 1 and 2 compared outcomes inside each week. When the condition comes from the previous day's result (“after an up day”), that method leans toward reversal even on random coin flips, so their “candidates” are not evidence. Version 3 measures the overall difference in rates with errors grouped in 28-day blocks, and asks whether tomorrow’s range beats a normal day’s, not today’s. Versions 1 and 2 stopped running on 24 November 2026; their results stay published for the record (version 2, version 1).

How to read it

The 2020–2026 history was already looked at before this plan was registered, so it is descriptive only. New evidence starts on 1 December 2026: each 1 December a cumulative snapshot is computed and frozen, and a test can only become a candidate once that sample covers 30 blocks of 28 days (late March 2029). A candidate means “worth a separate, costed test”, never an edge. Nothing is promoted automatically.
