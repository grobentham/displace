Displace Business

Custom research built on the same ledger: source-linked evidence, stated limits and checkpoints graded in public.

What a brief contains

  • The decision you are making, in one sentence.
  • Dated public evidence from World's sources: rules, filings, coverage, prices and official data.
  • How the evidence connects to your business, with documented and unproven links labelled.
  • What could change the conclusion, and dated checkpoints Displace will grade in public.

Examples of questions

  • Will a proposed US tariff rule take effect on its stated date, and which of my import categories does it name?
  • Is coverage of a supplier's country rising unusually, and from which outlets?
  • How did watch-list shares move after earlier filings of this kind?

Request research

Requests open a GitHub issue on the public Displace repository, so other people can read them. Use only public or non-sensitive information.
